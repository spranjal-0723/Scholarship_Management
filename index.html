💡 Understanding This Knowledge Graph — Node Roles, Traversal Pathways & Exploration Guide Click to expand / collapse
📌 Node Roles & Entity Semantics:
  • Student: Core matriculated student entity.
  • Scholarship: Grant fund with qualification criteria.
  • Application: Merit/need aid request submitted by student.
  • Fee: Tuition & institutional fee invoice item.
  • Payment: Transaction processed via payment method.
  • Disbursement: Released scholarship aid deposited into bank accounts.
🔍 Traversal Pathways & Exploration:
  • Scholarship Path: (Student)→(Application)→(Scholarship)→(Disbursement)
  • Tuition Flow: (Student)→(Fee)→(Payment)→(PaymentMethod)
  • Focus Node: Click any node to highlight 1-hop connections and dim others.
  • Node Inspector: Detailed properties and direct pointer links appear in the right drawer.
  • Pan & Zoom: Scroll to zoom, drag background to pan canvas, drag nodes to reposition.
